Spending Galaxy

Every category is a galaxy sized by what you spent (twice the dollars = twice the area); the ring around it is the budget, filled by how much of it is gone. Vendors are the stars inside, sized by spend. Zoom out and it all folds into one circle; zoom in and a category opens into its stars. Hold a circle, or a star, half a second to move it. Tap a dot in the legend to pick its colour. Pick a month or the year to date.

Tap a galaxy or a star.
Pinch, or Shift + scroll, to zoom · zoomed out = one circle for everything; zoom in and a category opens into its stars · one finger scrolls the page; to move the map: hold half a second on empty space then drag, two fingers, the edge sliders, or a mouse drag · hold half a second on a circle, or on a star once it shows, to move it (Arrange puts them back) · tap a circle or a star · keep zooming and each star shows its numbers.
