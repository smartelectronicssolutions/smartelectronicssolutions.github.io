How to read this: the ratio = BTC price ÷ ETH price. A signal fires only when the ratio touches a fresh high or low of its rolling window and the window spread clears the threshold. Ratio at a fresh low → ETH is outrunning BTC → consider rotating BTC → ETH; at a fresh high → the reverse. Every swap costs ~1% round-trip in fees, so the net edge is thin — treat alerts as “look,” not “trade now,” and let the weekly backtest prove a positive edge before committing real money.
