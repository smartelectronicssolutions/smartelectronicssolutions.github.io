Questions

What owners ask first

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. You talk to it by text like you would an employee. I set everything up and keep it running.

What if it gets something wrong?

It only answers from what you've told it. Anything it isn't sure about, it hands to you instead of guessing, and you can correct it any time by texting it.

Who sees my customers' information?

You do. Each business gets its own separate setup; your data isn't shared with or mixed into anyone else's, and it's yours if you ever leave.

Do I need a new website?

No. It adds to the site you have. If you don't have one, Your Tech Guy can build one.

Is there a contract?

Month to month after setup. It has to earn its keep.
